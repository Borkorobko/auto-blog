Warm-up before speed training

  • 5 minutes light jogging
  • Leg swings and hip mobility
  • Skipping or fast feet drills
  • 3 short accelerations at 60–80% speed

Best speed drills

  • 10 metre accelerations: 6 reps, rest 60–90 seconds.
  • 20 metre sprints: 4 reps, rest 2 minutes.
  • Shuttle runs: 3 sets of 5 reps.
  • Cone change-of-direction drill: 4 rounds of 20 seconds.

Weekly plan

Do speed training 2 times per week. Keep at least 48 hours between hard sprint sessions.

Common mistakes

  • Doing speed work while already tired.
  • Resting too little between sprints.
  • Training speed every day.
  • Ignoring sprint technique.

FAQ

How often should football players train speed?

Most players can train speed 1–2 times per week depending on matches and recovery.

Should speed training be done before or after gym?

Usually before heavy gym work, because speed training needs fresh legs.
